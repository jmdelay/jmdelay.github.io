## Data Science & Analytics

**Forecasting & Predictive Modeling**  
Anticipate demand, revenue, or customer behavior so you can plan ahead instead of react. Ideal for inventory, staffing, and budget planning.

**Dashboards & Reporting**  
Replace static spreadsheets with live, interactive dashboards you can use day-to-day. I build tools that fit your workflow, not the other way around.

**Data Cleaning & Pipeline Automation**  
Stop doing repetitive data work by hand. I automate the grunt work so your team can focus on what matters.

**Ad-hoc Analysis**  
Have a specific question you need answered with data? I dig in and give you a clear, actionable answer — no long-term contract required.

Deep experience with Medicare quality improvement, EHR data, prior authorization data, and clinical program ROI analysis. I can help health plans, providers, and vendors make sense of their data.

## Research Consulting

I also support researchers and small organizations with **research consulting** — so you can strengthen your methods and deliverables without hiring a full-time statistician.

- **Literature reviews & evidence synthesis** — Organize and summarize existing evidence to support grants, reports, or strategy.
- **Study design & power** — Choose the right design and sample size for surveys, experiments, or observational studies.
- **Survey design & analysis** — From question wording to analysis and reporting.
- **Grant support & manuscript prep** — Methods sections, analysis plans, and clear presentation of results for funders and journals.

*Ideal for:* academics, nonprofits, small research teams, and anyone who needs rigorous but practical research support.
