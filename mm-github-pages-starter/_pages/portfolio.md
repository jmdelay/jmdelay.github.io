## Nonprofit impact measurement

**Organization type:** Nonprofit — poverty alleviation programs
**Engagement type:** Survey design, data analysis, impact reporting

The organization needed credible evidence of program impact for funders and board reporting. They had no structured way to collect or analyze outcomes — just anecdotal feedback and a sense that the work was making a difference, but nothing they could put in front of a grant committee.

I designed the survey instrument from scratch: question structure, response scales, sampling approach, and distribution plan. Once responses came in, I cleaned and analyzed the data and produced a concise impact report with key metrics, visualizations, and a plain-language narrative that the organization could hand directly to funders and board members.

## Healthcare clinic — marketing campaign evaluation

**Organization type:** Local healthcare clinic
**Engagement type:** Before/after analysis, statistical significance testing, executive summary

The clinic had run a targeted marketing campaign and wanted to know whether it had actually worked. They had the numbers — patient volume before and after, some demographic data — but no way to distinguish a real signal from seasonal noise or random variation.

I analyzed before/after patient volume and mix, tested for statistically meaningful shifts using appropriate methods for the data they had, and summarized the findings in plain language. The deliverable was a short executive brief leadership could act on: did the campaign move the needle, and by how much.
